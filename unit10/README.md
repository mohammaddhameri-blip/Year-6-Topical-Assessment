# Unit 10 — It's a Mystery

This is the **Year 6 Topical Assessment teacher-testing build**. It provides interactive individual Reading (8 multiple-choice questions), Listening (original narrated community report with speed selection and 4 complete-sentence questions), Reading–Writing (2 subjective notice questions), dialogue choices and original dialogue writing, and Speaking (3 microphone recordings).

Open `index.html` over HTTPS; microphone recording requires user permission. Narration is currently played with the device's text-to-speech engine (not the separately prepared MP3 recording); voice quality and availability depend on the device. Audio transcripts are not displayed on the pupil's listening screen.

The pilot accepts `TST-###` tester codes. Text responses persist locally in localStorage and recorded speech in IndexedDB. Submitting **does not upload** work to the school, and **does not award TP levels or CEFR scores**. The end screen clearly states this. Do **not** use for official pupils until authenticated `Y6-###` pupil codes, secure storage, attempt controls, AI-assisted marking and teacher approval are implemented.

The private teacher blueprint and answer keys should not be committed to a public pupil-facing repository.
